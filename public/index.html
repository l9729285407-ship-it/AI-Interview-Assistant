AI Interview Preparation Assistant

A Streamlit web app that analyzes your resume, serves categorized interview questions, and scores your written answers using TF-IDF vectorization, cosine similarity and rule-based keyword matching — with a performance dashboard and weak-topic recommendations stored in SQLite.
